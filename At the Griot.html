A New Exhibit Opens: "Deep Roots: African Aestetic Response to the Banjo"

October 19, 2025

The Griot Museum of Black History opened its latest exhibit, “Honoring the Ancestors,” which explores themes of memory, migration, and cultural continuity. The opening ceremony featured local artists, poets, and historians.
